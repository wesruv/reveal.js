  • Was designed for "the Gates to Hell", he is at the top center of the doorway
  • The doorway depicts scenes from dante's inferno
  • The thinker (also known as the Poet in this context) has a seat in the top center
  • Some guesses for who the thinker are includes Dante, Rodin, or Adam (& Eve)

  • He is very muscular with large hands and fingers
  • Fun fact! Rodin was into Phrenology

Michaelangelo Was A Forger

Kinda... maybe

  • In his teens he was a starving artist in Florence, not getting much attention or sales
  • A copy of his of a sleeping cupid was buried in a vineyard and artificially aged to make it look like an ancient Roman artifact
  • It was sold by Baldassari del Milanese to an Italian Cardinal named Raffaele Riario
  • Before long the forgery was discovered
  • The seller (Milanese) was forced to pay back his cut of the sale price and was given the forgery
  • However Michaelangelo's sculpture was so impressivef to the Cardinal that he was invited to Rome
  • This kicked off his career, which shot so high that he could upset the Pope and not get killed
